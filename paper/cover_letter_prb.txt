Dear Editor,

We submit the revised manuscript for consideration as a Regular Article in
Physical Review B, together with Supplemental Material responding to the
referee report.

Summary of revisions (Major comments):

(1) Rigid-strain protocol — We added a four-corner fixed-cell ionic relaxation
    benchmark (Table S3) with inputs and analysis scripts archived in the
    repository. Main text and Discussion now separate periodic rigid S(n) from
    tetramer relaxed cross terms and state explicitly when conclusions are
    upper-bound vs. thermodynamic formation energies.

(2) Single dopant seed — We extended the alternate-seed validation to the full
    B/N/P strain grid (18 ENERGY tasks at seed 137; Table S4). This tests
    whether alpha signs and S rankings are placement-averaged trends rather than
    seed-42 artifacts.

(3) Mechanistic depth — New Table S5 quantifies dopant--cage nearest-neighbor
    distances and covalent-radius mismatch from archived XYZ structures; Discussion
    mechanistic synthesis now cites these numbers alongside PDOS and SDC audit data.

(4) Argumentation — We rewrote the S--alpha comparison to avoid over-claiming
    linear predictability, added a B vs. P stability ranking worked example at
    epsilon=0 vs. +3% strain, unified substitution-energy terminology, and
    clarified that S is a same-functional energy difference with largely
    cancelling systematic errors.

(5) Cutoff consistency — A matched 400 Ry single-point control for 6xC60 N @
    +3% strain (Table S2) is queued; N size-scaling sign change remains
    footnoted until that benchmark converges.

Minor comments: improved PRB/SI figure legibility (shared y-axis S4, n=4 S labels); Discussion/Limitations literature contrast (Wang2024, Lopez-Alcala2025, Silva2024); symbol and figure cleanup (PRB composite panel without inset;
    n=4 S annotations), compressed Methods validation table and Results electronic
    subsection, APS sentence-case references, and Supplemental Material citation
    per PRB guidelines.

Data availability: converged inputs/outputs and audit JSON as in the manuscript;
    https://github.com/chenxingqiang/graphullerene-strain-engineering

Prior submission: none to APS journals. Not a joint submission.

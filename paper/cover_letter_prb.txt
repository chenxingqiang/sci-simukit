Dear Editor,

We submit the enclosed manuscript for consideration as a Regular Article in Physical Review B.

Context. Strain and heteroatom substitution are routinely combined in two-dimensional molecular networks, yet many pipelines superpose separate strain-only and doping-only scans. Quasi-hexagonal C60 graphullerene provides a covalent model system to quantify the omitted total-energy cross term.

Key findings. We define a synergy order parameter S (meV/atom) from PBE+DFT-D3 on tetramers and periodic supercells. Opposite strain coefficients for N and B coexist with |S| up to ~32 meV/atom at +3% strain (largest for P despite small |alpha|). Protocol limits and validation benchmarks (ionic relaxation, alternate dopant seed, matched cutoff) are in Supplemental Material.

Fit for PRB. Condensed-matter materials design with reproducible CP2K archives and audit JSON. Transport-adjacent observables are in Supplemental Material only.

Prior submission: none to APS journals. Not a joint submission.

Data availability: see manuscript; https://github.com/chenxingqiang/graphullerene-strain-engineering

Xingqiang Chen (on behalf of all authors)

Dear Editor,

We submit the revised manuscript for consideration as a Regular Article in
Physical Review B in response to the referee report.

The contribution is a condensed-matter physics result, not a new computational
descriptor. Under mechanically constrained joint load on quasi-hexagonal C60
graphullerene, additive strain--dopant energetic screening fails: totals on the
joint E(epsilon, delta) landscape need not equal the sum of separate strain-only
and substitution-only increments. The bilinear cross term in a Taylor expansion
of total energy can reach meV-per-atom magnitudes large enough to change
high-throughput stability rankings for size-mismatched phosphorus, while linear
strain coefficients alpha do not reliably predict that omitted term. The matched
four-corner difference S is a finite-difference estimator of the mixed
second-order coupling---a diagnostic protocol, not the physical discovery.
Ionic relaxation nearly extinguishes |S|, indicating that the audited coupling
originates predominantly from stored elastic work under constrained load rather
than from an intrinsic electronic interaction term.

All former supplemental tables and figures are incorporated in the main text;
no separate Supplemental Material file accompanies this revision.

Summary of revisions (Major comments):

(1) Rigid-strain protocol — Rigid-cell alpha and S are upper bounds for
    mechanically constrained loading; ionic-relaxation tetramer benchmarks
    (Table III, 4/4 corners) show sign reversal and orders-of-magnitude
    suppression upon stress release. The main text separates periodic rigid
    S(n) from tetramer relaxed cross terms and states that E_sub is not a
    thermodynamic formation enthalpy.

(2) Alternate dopant placement — Table IV documents reference and alternate
    seed-137 maps (18/18 PBE+D3 rigid ENERGY tasks on the tetramer grid;
    16/16 periodic placement audits on seeds 137 and 271). Periodic |S|
    magnitudes remain configuration-specific; ranking trends are reported
    with placement caveats.

(3) Mechanistic depth — Table V and Discussion IV.B give a primary /
    secondary / tertiary hierarchy for phosphorus (geometric misfit strain,
    electronic redistribution, pi-DOS morphology). Electronic structure
    responds to the coupling rather than controlling it.

(4) Argumentation — The S--alpha comparison is qualitative only
    (concentration and boundary mismatch). Stability language is scoped to
    relative margins among comparable chemistries. Discussion IV is
    restructured (main finding, mechanism, literature, limitations, outlook)
    with PRB-appropriate passive tone.

(5) Robustness — Table sigma_S reports an error budget (SCF, cutoff, vdW,
    ionic relaxation, k-sampling, XC sensitivity); SCAN/r2SCAN and dense-k
    benchmarks remain honestly pending in Limitations.

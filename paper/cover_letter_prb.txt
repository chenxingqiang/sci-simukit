Dear Editor,

We submit the revised manuscript for consideration as a Regular Article in
Physical Review B in response to the referee report. All former supplemental

revision.

Summary of revisions (Major comments):

(1) Rigid-strain protocol — We state explicitly that rigid-cell $\alpha$ and
    $\mathcal{S}$ are upper bounds until ionic-relaxation and alternate-placement
    benchmarks complete (Tables~III--IV). Table~III defines a four-corner
    fixed-cell GEO\_OPT benchmark (pristine and $P$ at $\epsilon{=}0$ and $+3$\%;
    4/4 corners converged; $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{relaxed}}=-2.28$ vs.\ rigid $+0.96$~meV/atom,
    sign not preserved; cross-functional legacy PBE vs.\ PBE+D3). The main text separates periodic rigid $\mathcal{S}(n)$ from
    from tetramer relaxed cross terms and clarifies that $E_{\mathrm{sub}}$ is not
    a thermodynamic formation enthalpy.

(2) Alternate dopant placement — Table~IV documents reference-placement
    $\mathcal{S}_{+3\%}^{\mathrm{tet}}$ on the archived legacy-PBE grid and a
    completed alternate seed-137 map (18/18 PBE+D3 rigid ENERGY tasks): N $\alpha$
    reverses sign vs.\ the reference placement; P $|\alpha|$ is placement-dominated
    ($\sim 10^3$~meV/\%); $\mathcal{S}_{+3\%}^{\mathrm{tet}}$ remains $\mathcal{O}(10)$~meV/atom
    on the alternate map (cross-functional comparison to legacy-PBE reference column
    remains qualitative). Periodic $\mathcal{S}$ magnitudes remain
    configuration-specific; periodic placement audits (seeds 137 and 271; 16/16 rigid single-points converged; both preserve P$>$B$>$N $|\mathcal{S}|$ ranking at $+3$\%) are complete. Population-strain Hirshfeld tasks remain in the PRB queue.

(3) Mechanistic depth — Table~V quantifies dopant--cage nearest-neighbor
    distances and covalent-radius mismatch from archived XYZ structures
    (geometric metrics only; Mayer bond order and Bader charges are not
    computed). Discussion mechanistic synthesis cites these numbers together
    with gap-vs-strain slopes (main Fig.~1a), $\pi$-DOS panels (main Fig.~1b;
    standalone Fig.~2), and the fifteen-point synergy audit.

(4) Argumentation — We rewrote the $\mathcal{S}$--$\alpha$ comparison as a
    qualitative sanity check only (concentration mismatch), tightened stability
    language to \emph{relative} margins among comparable chemistries (without
    claiming reordering of widely separated $E_{\mathrm{sub}}$), and clarified
    that $\mathcal{S}$ is a same-functional energy difference with largely
    cancelling systematic errors.

(5) Cutoff consistency — A matched 400~Ry single-point control for
    $6\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$ N @ $+3$\% strain (Table~II; pending) is
    documented; the reported N $\mathcal{S}(n)$ sign change between $n=6$ and
    $n=8$ is excluded from design rules until the cutoff400 benchmark converges
    (Table~III--IV tetramer controls are complete).

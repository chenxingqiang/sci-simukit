Dear Editor,

We submit the revised manuscript for consideration as a Regular Article in
Physical Review B, together with Supplemental Material responding to the
referee report.

Summary of revisions (Major comments):

(1) Rigid-strain protocol — We state explicitly that rigid-cell $\alpha$ and
    $\mathcal{S}$ are upper bounds until ionic relaxation completes. Table~S3
    defines a four-corner fixed-cell GEO\_OPT benchmark for the tetramer
    $P@+3$\% synergy corner (relaxed energies pending; rigid reference values
    archived). The main text separates periodic rigid $\mathcal{S}(n)$ from
    tetramer relaxed cross terms and clarifies that $E_{\mathrm{sub}}$ is not
    a thermodynamic formation enthalpy.

(2) Single dopant seed — Table~S4 documents an alternate seed~137 tetramer
    strain grid (18 ENERGY tasks; pending) to test whether $\alpha$ signs and
    $|\mathcal{S}|$ rankings at $+3$\% are robust to dopant placement. Periodic
    $\mathcal{S}$ claims remain on the seed~42 protocol until seed~137 periodic
    points are added.

(3) Mechanistic depth — Table~S5 quantifies dopant--cage nearest-neighbor
    distances and covalent-radius mismatch from archived XYZ structures
    (geometric metrics only; Mayer bond order and Bader charges are not
    computed). Discussion mechanistic synthesis cites these numbers together
    with gap-vs-strain slopes (main Fig.~1a), $\pi$-DOS panels (main Fig.~1b;
    Supplemental Fig.~S4), and the fifteen-point synergy audit.

(4) Argumentation — We rewrote the $\mathcal{S}$--$\alpha$ comparison as a
    qualitative sanity check only (concentration mismatch), tightened stability
    language to \emph{relative} margins among comparable chemistries (without
    claiming reordering of widely separated $E_{\mathrm{sub}}$), and clarified
    that $\mathcal{S}$ is a same-functional energy difference with largely
    cancelling systematic errors.

(5) Cutoff consistency — A matched 400~Ry single-point control for
    $6\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$ N @ $+3$\% strain (Table~S2; pending) is
    documented; the reported N $\mathcal{S}(n)$ sign change between $n=6$ and
    $n=8$ is excluded from design rules until that benchmark and Tables~S3--S4
    converge.

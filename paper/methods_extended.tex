\subsubsection{Ionic-relaxation benchmark}
\label{sec:methods_relax}
Two boundary conditions are kept separate. The periodic $n{=}1$ $P$ four-corner fixed-cell BFGS protocol (force thresholds $4.5\times10^{-4}$ and $3.0\times10^{-4}$~Ha/bohr, ${\sim}0.023$ and ${\sim}0.015$~eV/\AA; displacement thresholds $3.0\times10^{-3}$ and $1.5\times10^{-3}$~bohr) is the constrained diagnostic reported in Fig.~\ref{fig:synergy}(b): ionic relaxation suppresses the cross term to the reporting floor. Rigid $|\mathcal{S}|$ on this path is an upper-end protocol estimate relative to that fully relaxed fixed-cell protocol, not a universal bound on every substrate, adhesion, or load path.
The tetramer pristine/$P$ four-corner BFGS grid does \emph{not} affine-scale molecular coordinates (only the vacuum box changes). Table~\ref{tab:relax} is therefore a distinct boundary condition, not a retention ratio for the periodic series: the cross term reverses sign and increases in magnitude ($+1.19\to-2.28$~meV/atom).
These thresholds are tighter than typical 0.02~eV/\AA\ defaults because relaxation can drive $|\mathcal{S}|$ toward zero and must be resolved at sub-meV/atom scale.

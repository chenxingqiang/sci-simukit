\subsubsection{Ionic-relaxation benchmark}
\label{sec:methods_relax}
Fixed-cell BFGS geometry optimization is performed on the tetramer pristine/$P$ corners at $\epsilon{=}0$ and $+3$\% (force thresholds $4.5\times10^{-4}$ and $3.0\times10^{-4}$~Ha/bohr, ${\sim}0.023$ and ${\sim}0.015$~eV/\AA; displacement thresholds $3.0\times10^{-3}$ and $1.5\times10^{-3}$~bohr).
These thresholds are tighter than typical 0.02~eV/\AA\ defaults because relaxation can drive $|\mathcal{S}|$ toward zero and must be resolved at sub-meV/atom scale.
A matched-functional rigid PBE+D3 control at the relaxed coordinates quantifies how $\mathcal{S}$ changes upon relaxation (Table~\ref{tab:relax}).
The same BFGS thresholds are used for the periodic $n{=}1$ P four-corner relaxation reported in Fig.~\ref{fig:synergy}(b).

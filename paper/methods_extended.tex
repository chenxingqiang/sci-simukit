\subsubsection{DFT calculator settings}
\label{sec:methods_dft_detail}
Periodic total-energy and electronic-structure tasks use CP2K~\cite{cp2k2025} with PBE~\cite{Perdew1996generalized} and Grimme DFT-D3 (Becke--Johnson damping)~\cite{Grimme2011effect}.
Tetramer $\alpha$ and $E_{\mathrm{sub}}$ in Table~\ref{tab:I} come from the archived rigid tetramer grid (legacy PBE without D3).
A matched PBE+D3 rigid re-evaluation of the reference placement map (seed~42; 24 tasks at 400~Ry, $10^{-6}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$ SCF) is underway (Table~\ref{tab:II}); published $\alpha$ values are not replaced until that grid converges.
Unless stated otherwise, biaxial strain scales the in-plane lattice vectors of the pristine equilibrium cell at fixed fractional coordinates (rigid-strain protocol).
The plane-wave cutoff is 400~Ry for $n\leq 4$ and 350~Ry for $n\geq 6$ (Table~\ref{tab:II}); the atom-centered basis (DZVP-MOLOPT-SR-GTH) is unchanged across $n$.
Tetramer models ($4\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$, 240 atoms) use B/N/P substitution at nominal 5\% (12--13 dopant sites from archived coordinates).
Periodic supercells place one substituent per C$_{60}$ unit ($n=1$--$8$), giving $n$ dopants among $60n$ lattice atoms (${\sim}1.67$~at.\% for all $n$).

\subsubsection{Alternate-placement benchmark (Table~\ref{tab:IV})}
\label{sec:methods_s4_seed}
The \emph{reference} placement in Table~\ref{tab:IV} is the archived rigid tetramer map underlying Table~\ref{tab:I} (legacy PBE without D3).
The \emph{alternate} placement repeats the full B/N/P strain grid ($\epsilon\in\{-5,-2.5,0,+2.5,+3,+5\}$\%; 18 rigid single-point tasks) with an independently rerolled substitution map at the same nominal 5\% doping level (pseudorandom seed~137; public archive).
Reference-placement $\mathcal{S}_{+3\%}^{\mathrm{tet}}$ for B/N/P is tabulated in Table~\ref{tab:IV} and Sec.~\ref{sec:synergy}; periodic $\mathcal{S}(n)$ is not inferred from either tetramer map.
Alternate tasks use PBE+D3 rigid single-point total-energy evaluations at 400~Ry with an SCF convergence threshold of $10^{-6}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$; they are \emph{not} on the same exchange-correlation treatment as the reference $\alpha$ column, so only within-placement trends are compared once the grid converges.
At periodic $n{=}4$, two independent site maps repeat the four-corner Eq.~\eqref{eq:synergy_order} audit (pristine and B/N/P at $\epsilon{=}0$ and $+3$\%; eight energies per map, 16 total) under the same PBE+D3 rigid protocol as the fifteen-point size-scaling grid; rankings of $|\mathcal{S}|$ at $+3$\% are compared to the production reference map in Table~\ref{tab:Sgrid}. Fixed pseudorandom seeds 137 and 271 identify the alternate maps in the archive; the production map uses seed~42.
Where alternate-grid rigid single-points stall in SCF, the convergence threshold may be relaxed to $10^{-5}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$ for that task.
The same relaxation applies to reference-placement modernization tasks when inner OT reaches 300 steps or outer SCF fails to converge (up to 21 outer cycles at $10^{-6}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$).

\subsubsection{Ionic-relaxation benchmark (Table~\ref{tab:III})}
\label{sec:methods_s3_relax}
The four tetramer corners in Table~\ref{tab:III} are pristine and $P$-substituted cells at $\epsilon{=}0$ and $+3$\% biaxial strain (the Eq.~\eqref{eq:synergy_order} grid for $P$).
They use fixed-cell geometry optimization with BFGS (maximum 300 ionic steps for pristine corners and 450 for $P$-substituted corners) at prescribed in-plane cell vectors; only fractional coordinates relax while lattice vectors remain fixed.
BFGS stops at 300 steps or when the maximum and RMS force components fall below $4.5\times10^{-4}$ and $3.0\times10^{-4}$~Ha/bohr, or when the maximum and RMS atomic displacements fall below $3.0\times10^{-3}$ and $1.5\times10^{-3}$~bohr, respectively.
These tasks use the same PBE+D3 settings as periodic audits: 400~Ry plane-wave cutoff, DZVP-MOLOPT-SR-GTH basis, and an SCF convergence threshold of $10^{-6}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$ (pristine) or $10^{-5}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$ ($P$-substituted) with OT--DIIS (up to 300 inner SCF iterations per ionic step and 20--40 outer SCF cycles) before updating the density for the next BFGS displacement.
Rigid reference energies in Table~\ref{tab:III} remain the archived legacy-PBE (no D3) single-point grid; relaxed corners use PBE+D3, so the legacy cross-functional row is sign-qualitative only.
A matched-functional control recomputes the same four corners as rigid PBE+D3 single points at the relaxed fractional coordinates (400~Ry, $10^{-6}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$ SCF, no ionic steps); quantitative retention is read from the matched-functional row in Table~\ref{tab:III}, while the legacy-PBE rigid row remains for comparability with the archived tetramer grid.


\subsubsection{Population strain paths ($n{=}1$ B/N/P)}
\label{sec:methods_population}
Eighteen rigid single-point tasks on the $n{=}1$ B-, N-, and P-substituted periodic supercells scan Hirshfeld atomic charges along $\epsilon\in\{-5,-2.5,0,+2.5,+3,+5\}$\% (Table~\ref{tab:II}); Mayer bond orders are extracted in post-processing from converged wavefunctions.
These tasks use the same PBE+D3, 400~Ry, and $10^{-6}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$ SCF settings as the periodic size-scaling grid; doped $n{=}1$ cells have odd electron counts, so spin-polarized KS (\texttt{LSD .TRUE.}) is enabled (unlike the closed-shell size-scaling ENERGY tasks).
They complement geometric $\bar{d}$ metrics in Table~\ref{tab:V}.

\subsubsection{Periodic ionic-relaxation benchmark ($n{=}1$ P)}
\label{sec:methods_periodic_relax}
Fixed-cell geometry optimization on the periodic $n{=}1$ P-substitution four corners (pristine and $P$ at $\epsilon{=}0$ and $+3$\%; PBE+D3, 400~Ry) uses the same BFGS thresholds as Table~\ref{tab:III}.
Inputs are generated from the converged rigid single-point cells in the size-scaling archive; all four corners converged ($\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{relaxed}}\ll \mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{rigid}}$; Sec.~\ref{sec:relax_checkpoint})---do not rescale tetramer Table~\ref{tab:III} benchmarks to periodic cells.

\subsubsection{Marcus vertical step and post-processing}
\label{sec:methods_marcus}
Charged-state geometry optimizations supply adiabatic ionization potentials and electron affinities ($E_{\mathrm{opt}}^{\pm}$).
Eight vertical single-point energy evaluations compute $E_{\mathrm{vert}}^{\pm}$ at the \emph{neutral}-geometry coordinates with the charged UKS settings of the relaxed state, following Ref.~\citenum{Capobianco2024electron}; $\lambda^{\pm}=E_{\mathrm{vert}}^{\pm}-E_{\mathrm{opt}}^{\pm}$ (Supplemental Material).
For near-gap B doping, negative $\lambda^{-}$ from the vertical step at fixed neutral coordinates is treated as a semilocal artifact and excluded from rate estimates.
$\pi$-projected densities of states use a 0.05~eV Gaussian broadening aligned to the Fermi level (Fig.~\ref{fig:pdos}).
Inverse participation ratios and hopping integrals $J$ are defined in Sec.~\ref{sec:methods}; verified IPR and $J$ values accompany the open data release (not used to infer $\mathcal{S}$).

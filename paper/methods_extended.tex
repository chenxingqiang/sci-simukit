\subsubsection{DFT calculator settings}
\label{sec:methods_dft_detail}
Periodic total-energy and electronic-structure tasks use CP2K~\cite{cp2k2025} with PBE~\cite{Perdew1996generalized} and Grimme DFT-D3 (Becke--Johnson damping)~\cite{Grimme2011effect}.
Tetramer $\alpha$ and $E_{\mathrm{sub}}$ in Table~\ref{tab:S1} come from the archived rigid tetramer grid (legacy PBE without D3).
Unless stated otherwise, biaxial strain scales the in-plane lattice vectors of the pristine equilibrium cell at fixed fractional coordinates (rigid-strain protocol).
The plane-wave cutoff is 400~Ry for $n\leq 4$ and 350~Ry for $n\geq 6$ (Table~\ref{tab:S2}); the atom-centered basis (DZVP-MOLOPT-SR-GTH) is unchanged across $n$.
Tetramer models ($4\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$, 240 atoms) use B/N/P substitution at nominal 5\% (12--13 dopant sites from archived coordinates).
Periodic supercells place one substituent per C$_{60}$ unit ($n=1$--$8$), giving $n$ dopants among $60n$ lattice atoms (${\sim}1.67$~at.\% for all $n$).

\subsubsection{Ionic-relaxation benchmark (Table~\ref{tab:S3})}
\label{sec:methods_s3_relax}
The four tetramer corners in Table~\ref{tab:S3} use \texttt{RUN\_TYPE GEO\_OPT} with BFGS (maximum 300 ionic steps) at prescribed biaxial cell vectors ($\epsilon{=}0$ or $+3$\%); only fractional coordinates relax while lattice vectors remain fixed.
These tasks use the same PBE+D3 settings as periodic audits: 400~Ry plane-wave cutoff, DZVP-MOLOPT-SR-GTH basis, and $\mathrm{EPS\_SCF}=10^{-6}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$.
Rigid reference energies in Table~\ref{tab:S3} remain the archived legacy-PBE (no D3) single-point grid; relaxed and rigid corners therefore differ in exchange-correlation treatment, and only sign retention of $\mathcal{S}$ is interpreted.

\subsubsection{Marcus vertical step and post-processing}
\label{sec:methods_marcus}
Charged-state geometry optimizations supply adiabatic ionization potentials and electron affinities ($E_{\mathrm{opt}}^{\pm}$).
Eight vertical single-point energy evaluations compute $E_{\mathrm{vert}}^{\pm}$ at the \emph{neutral}-geometry coordinates with the charged UKS settings of the relaxed state, following Ref.~\citenum{Capobianco2024electron}; $\lambda^{\pm}=E_{\mathrm{vert}}^{\pm}-E_{\mathrm{opt}}^{\pm}$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:S2}).
$\pi$-projected densities of states use a 0.05~eV Gaussian broadening aligned to the Fermi level (Fig.~\ref{fig:S1}).
Inverse participation ratios and hopping integrals $J$ are defined in Sec.~\ref{sec:methods} and Fig.~\ref{fig:S3}; numerical values are listed in the Data Availability repository.

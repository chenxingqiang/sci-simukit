\textbf{Non-additive coupling metric.}
We quantify strain--doping cross terms with the synergy order parameter $\mathcal{S}$:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{S}(\epsilon,\delta)
= \Delta E(\epsilon,\delta) - \bigl[\Delta E(\epsilon,0) + \Delta E(0,\delta)\bigr],
\label{eq:synergy_order}
\end{equation}
where $\epsilon$ is biaxial strain, $\delta\in\{B,N,P\}$, and $\Delta E(\epsilon,\delta)$ is the energy per atom relative to the pristine, zero-strain reference at the same supercell size $n\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$.
The bracketed term is the \emph{additive} prediction from separate strain-only and doping-only scans; deviations from zero measure non-additive coupling.
Additive superposition gives $\mathcal{S}=0$; $\mathcal{S}<0$ indicates cooperative stabilization beyond the two-scan sum, whereas $\mathcal{S}>0$ indicates cooperative destabilization.

To first order in strain, $\mathcal{S}(\epsilon,\delta)\approx(\alpha_\delta-\alpha_0)\,\epsilon$ when $\alpha_\delta=\mathrm{d}E/\mathrm{d}\epsilon$ is evaluated for the doped cell and $\alpha_0$ for the pristine reference at the same $n$ (rigid-strain protocol); this links the mechanical coefficient $\alpha$ to the cross term at small $\epsilon$.

Periodic-cell $\mathcal{S}$ values are obtained from converged CP2K outputs with \texttt{c/simukit-sdc} (Supplemental Material Table~S2).
The fifteen-point $+3$\% strain audit is archived as \texttt{experiments/analysis/sdc/sdc\_exp10\_synergy\_audit.json} (\texttt{synergy\_S\_meV\_per\_atom}), refreshed by \texttt{experiments/post\_exp10\_converged.sh} after each converged periodic task.
We report $\mathcal{S}$ for total energy only; an analogous gap cross term $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{gap}}$ is not computed here---main-text panel~(a) shows that gap slopes need not track $|\mathcal{S}|$.

\textbf{Localization and coupling (Supplemental Material Fig.~S6).}
Following Capobianco \emph{et al.}~\cite{Capobianco2024electron}, we report the inverse participation ratio (IPR) of the charged-state frontier orbital and the nearest-neighbor electronic coupling $J$ from tetramer dimer fragments.
IPR follows Supporting Information Eq.~(S8), $N\sum_i|\psi_i|^4/(\sum_i|\psi_i|^2)^2$, on a Mulliken-projected orbital grid; $J$ is estimated from the ground--first-excited splitting of the anionic dimer at fixed interfullerene geometry (two-state model).
These observables are computed at the same PBE+D3 level as the energy scans and are \emph{not} directly comparable in absolute value to the Koopmans-compliant $J\simeq 75$--$81$~meV reported for pristine qHP networks~\cite{Capobianco2024electron}, but they provide an internally consistent trend under coupled $(\epsilon,\delta)$.

\textbf{Polaron binding (Supplemental Material).}
Marcus reorganization energies follow the vertical--adiabatic decomposition~\cite{Capobianco2024electron}:
$\lambda^{\pm}=E_{\mathrm{vert}}^{\pm}-E_{\mathrm{opt}}^{\pm}$, with separate internal ($\lambda^{\pm}_{\mathrm{loc}}$) and environmental ($\lambda^{\pm}_{\mathrm{env}}$) contributions when geometry subsets are relaxed.
Charged-state relaxations and vertical single-points are documented in Supplemental Material Fig.~S5.
Vertical ENERGY inputs at the neutral $q=0$ geometry are generated by \texttt{experiments/exp\_9\_charged\_polaron/generate\_vertical\_sp.py}; adiabatic IP/EA and $\lambda^{\pm}$ are audited in \texttt{experiments/analysis/exp9\_polaron\_verification.json} (\texttt{analyze\_exp9\_polaron.py}); twelve charged GEO\_OPT supply adiabatic IP/EA in the audit JSON (vertical SP batch in progress at revision). \texttt{post\_exp9\_converged.sh} refreshes the audit JSON and SI Fig.~S5 via \texttt{paper/figures/render\_si\_figures.sh}.

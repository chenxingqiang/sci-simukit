\subsubsection{Non-additive coupling metric}
We quantify strain--doping cross terms with the synergy order parameter $\mathcal{S}$:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{S}(\epsilon,\delta)
= \Delta E(\epsilon,\delta) - \bigl[\Delta E(\epsilon,0) + \Delta E(0,\delta)\bigr],
\label{eq:synergy_order}
\end{equation}
where $\epsilon$ is biaxial strain, $\delta\in\{B,N,P\}$, and $\Delta E(\epsilon,\delta)$ is the energy per atom relative to the pristine, zero-strain reference at the same supercell size $n\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$.
The bracketed term is the \emph{additive} prediction from separate strain-only and doping-only scans; deviations from zero measure non-additive coupling.
Additive superposition gives $\mathcal{S}=0$; $\mathcal{S}<0$ indicates cooperative stabilization beyond the two-scan sum, whereas $\mathcal{S}>0$ indicates cooperative destabilization.

\paragraph{Relation to interaction and formation energies.}
Algebraically, Eq.~\eqref{eq:synergy_order} has the form of an interaction (coupling) energy, and we claim no novelty in that construction.
Its distinct role is \emph{dimensional}: a formation or substitution energy subtracts a single reference state, and a binding or interaction energy measures the coupling of one fixed pair of fragments, whereas $\mathcal{S}$ is a symmetric \emph{double-reference cross term} over the four corners of the two-dimensional $(\epsilon,\delta)$ control space that is spanned by high-throughput screening.
It therefore quantifies a quantity none of the pairwise energies provide: the error incurred when total energies under joint load are assembled additively from separate strain-only and doping-only scans (Sec.~\ref{sec:synergy}).
Table~\ref{tab:descriptor} contrasts $\mathcal{S}$ with common stability and electronic descriptors on these axes.
Consistent with this purely energetic definition, $|\mathcal{S}|$ is \emph{not} reducible to a single geometric ($\Delta\bar{d}$) or charge ($\Delta q$) channel (Sec.~\ref{sec:mechanistic}); it is this irreducibility, rather than any new functional form, that motivates reporting $\mathcal{S}$ as a coupling descriptor for the joint design space.

When $\alpha_\delta=\mathrm{d}E/\mathrm{d}\epsilon$ denotes the \emph{total}-cell linear strain coefficient (meV per 1\% strain) at fixed $n$, a first-order estimate of the per-atom cross term is $\mathcal{S}(\epsilon,\delta)\approx(\alpha_\delta-\alpha_0)\,\epsilon/N_{\mathrm{atoms}}$ at small $\epsilon$ under the rigid-strain protocol; tetramer and periodic $n$ use different $N_{\mathrm{atoms}}$ and dopant concentrations, so this link is not used quantitatively in the periodic audit (Discussion, argument scope).

Periodic-cell $\mathcal{S}$ values are extracted from CP2K total energies (Table~\ref{tab:II}); the fifteen-point $+3$\% strain audit accompanies the open data release.
We report $\mathcal{S}$ for total energy only.
By the same four-corner construction one may define $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{gap}}=E_g(\epsilon,\delta)-[E_g(\epsilon,0)+E_g(0,\delta)-E_g(0,0)]$ (and analogously $\mathcal{S}_{J}$, $\mathcal{S}_{\lambda}$ for hopping or reorganization energies); these observables are \emph{not} tabulated here---panel~(a) of Fig.~\ref{fig:main} shows that gap slopes need not track $|\mathcal{S}|$.

\paragraph{Attribution framework (total energy).}
Conceptually, $\mathcal{S}=\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{geom}}+\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{el}}+\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{vdW}}$, where $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{geom}}$ collects nonlinear elastic work from bond-angle and cage-shape changes beyond first-order strain, $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{el}}$ from charge-transfer and orbital-rehybridization nonlinearities, and $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{vdW}}$ from the Grimme D3 cross term.
On the matched-functional tetramer $P$ rigid four-corner grid, extracting D3 dispersion energies gives $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{vdW}}\approx +0.33$~meV/atom---negligible versus $|\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{rigid}}^{\mathrm{PBE+D3}}|\approx 1.2$~meV/atom (Table~\ref{tab:III}).
Corner-resolved $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{vdW}}$ for B/N dopants and periodic cells beyond the matched tetramer $P$ grid remain open; Table~\ref{tab:V} ($\bar{d}$, $\Delta r_{\mathrm{cov}}$) and Hirshfeld population on the $n{=}1$ B/N/P strain paths (Sec.~\ref{sec:methods_population}; Table~\ref{tab:II}) proxy geometric vs.\ electronic channels in Discussion.

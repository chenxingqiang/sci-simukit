\subsection{Synergy order parameter}
We quantify strain--doping cross terms with the synergy order parameter $\mathcal{S}$:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{S}(\epsilon,\delta)
= \Delta E(\epsilon,\delta) - \bigl[\Delta E(\epsilon,0) + \Delta E(0,\delta)\bigr],
\label{eq:synergy_order}
\end{equation}
where $\epsilon$ is biaxial strain, $\delta\in\{\mathrm{B},\mathrm{N},\mathrm{P}\}$, and
$\Delta E(\epsilon,\delta)=\bigl[E(\epsilon,\delta)-E(0,\mathrm{pristine})\bigr]/N_{\mathrm{atoms}}$
is the energy per atom relative to the pristine, zero-strain reference at the same supercell size $n\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$.
The bracketed term is the additive prediction from separate strain-only and doping-only scans; $\mathcal{S}=0$ under perfect additivity, $\mathcal{S}<0$ indicates cooperative stabilization, and $\mathcal{S}>0$ indicates that the coupled corner is less stable than the additive prediction.
Periodic $\mathcal{S}$ values are taken from the $+3$\% size-scaling grid (Table~\ref{tab:IV}).

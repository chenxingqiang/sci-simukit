\subsection{Strain--dopant coupling energy}
\label{sec:methods_coupling}

$\mathcal{S}_\delta(\epsilon)$ is the discrete strain--substitution interaction for a fixed dopant species $\delta\in\{\mathrm{B},\mathrm{N},\mathrm{P}\}$ at $+3$\% load.
It is not a continuum derivative $\partial^2 E/\partial\epsilon\,\partial\delta$, not an order parameter, and not interchangeable with a mixed derivative unless a local composition coordinate is introduced and higher-order remainders are negligible (Supplemental Material, Table~\ref{S-tab:descriptor} and Sec.~\ref{S-sec:appendix_fd}).
B, N, and P are distinct chemical labels, not three numerical points on one continuous $\delta$ axis.

\begin{equation}
\mathcal{S}_\delta(\epsilon)
= \Delta E(\epsilon,\delta) - \bigl[\Delta E(\epsilon,0) + \Delta E(0,\delta)\bigr],
\label{eq:synergy_order}
\end{equation}
with
$\Delta E(\epsilon,\delta)=\bigl[E(\epsilon,\delta)-E(0,\mathrm{pristine})\bigr]/N_{\mathrm{atoms}}$
and $\delta{=}0$ denoting the pristine reference at the same supercell size $n\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$.
Equivalently, on corners $(0,0)$, $(\Delta\epsilon,0)$, $(0,\Delta\delta)$, $(\Delta\epsilon,\Delta\delta)$,
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{S}_\delta(\epsilon)=\frac{E(\epsilon,\delta)-E(\epsilon,0)-E(0,\delta)+E(0,0)}{N_{\mathrm{atoms}}},
\label{eq:four_corner}
\end{equation}
which depends only on the four corner totals (reference independence).
The four corner totals are evaluated on the same neutral Born--Oppenheimer surface; the stencil is not a charged-defect or polaron-rate estimator.

\paragraph{Why a four-point stencil?}
Eqs.~\eqref{eq:synergy_order}--\eqref{eq:four_corner} are the minimal rectangular stencil that uses four DFT totals, is second-order accurate in the bilinear term, cancels first-order increments and a shared offset, and equals the additive-screening bias by construction.
Asymmetric three-point schemes retain linear contamination; other estimators are compared in Supplemental Material, Sec.~\ref{S-sec:why_four}.

\paragraph{Finite-difference link after a local composition coordinate.}
The species label $\delta$ is discrete.
A mixed derivative $\partial^2 e/\partial\epsilon\,\partial x$ is defined only after a local continuous composition coordinate $x$ is introduced at that fixed species (main text, Eq.~\eqref{eq:taylor_coupling}).
Expanding each corner total in $(\epsilon,x)$ to second order and forming Eq.~\eqref{eq:four_corner} cancels the first-order increments identically (Supplemental Material, Sec.~\ref{S-sec:four_corner_deriv}), yielding
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{S}_\delta(\epsilon)\approx
\frac{\partial^2 e}{\partial\epsilon\,\partial x}\,
\Delta\epsilon\,\Delta x
+ \mathcal{O}(\Delta\epsilon^2,\Delta x^2),
\label{eq:mixed_derivative}
\end{equation}
where $\Delta x$ is a formal step of that local coordinate, not a spacing that places B, N, and P on one axis.
Eq.~\eqref{eq:mixed_derivative} is therefore not $\partial^2 E/\partial\epsilon\,\partial\delta$ with B/N/P treated as numerical points of a shared $\delta$.
At finite $+3$\% biaxial load and discrete B/N/P labels, higher-order terms in Eq.~\eqref{eq:mixed_derivative} are not separately resolved on the production periodic map; multi-amplitude strain audits ($\epsilon=1$--$3$\%) are targeted sensitivity tests (Limitations).
Throughout the main text, ``nonlinear coupling'' refers to a nonzero discrete strain--substitution interaction estimated by $\mathcal{S}_\delta(\epsilon)$, not to $\mathcal{S}$ itself as a fundamental constant and not to a continuum mixed derivative along a shared dopant axis.

$\mathcal{S}$ is reported in meV per atom and is invariant under a uniform energy offset shared by all four corners (Supplemental Material, Sec.~\ref{S-sec:symmetry_gauge}).

\paragraph{Error propagation.}
If each corner carries uncorrelated equal per-atom uncertainty $\sigma_e$,
\begin{equation}
\sigma_{\mathcal{S}}^2 = 4\sigma_e^2,
\qquad
\sigma_{\mathcal{S}} = 2\sigma_e,
\label{eq:sigma_propagation}
\end{equation}
from the four-term difference in Eq.~\eqref{eq:four_corner} (derivation: Supplemental Material, Sec.~\ref{S-sec:error_prop}).
Table~\ref{tab:sigma_S} lists contributions to the reporting band; we use $\sigma_{\mathcal{S}}\lesssim 2$~meV/atom as a conservative protocol floor on the $n\leq 4$ grid.
$\mathcal{S}<0$ ($>0$) means the coupled corner is more (less) stable than the additive estimate.
Additive-failure magnitude is
\begin{equation}
\eta \equiv \frac{|\mathcal{S}|}{|\Delta E_{\mathrm{coupled}}|},
\label{eq:additive_fraction}
\end{equation}
where $\Delta E_{\mathrm{coupled}}=(E(\epsilon,\delta)-E(0,0))/N_{\mathrm{atoms}}$.

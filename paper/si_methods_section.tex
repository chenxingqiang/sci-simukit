\section*{Computational Methods (full protocol)}

All total-energy and electronic-structure calculations use CP2K~\cite{VandeVondele2005quickstep} with PBE~\cite{Perdew1996generalized} and Grimme DFT-D3 (Becke--Johnson damping)~\cite{Grimme2011effect}, GTH pseudopotentials, and the DZVP-MOLOPT-SR-GTH basis.
Unless stated otherwise, biaxial strain on \emph{periodic} cells scales the in-plane lattice vectors of the pristine equilibrium cell at fixed fractional coordinates. That constrained protocol is a fixed-coordinate diagnostic (epitaxial clamp, strong adhesion, or ultrafast load). Rigid $|\mathcal{S}|$ on this path is an upper-end protocol estimate relative to that fully relaxed fixed-cell protocol, not a universal bound on every constraint and not an equilibrium free-energy difference.
Two ionic-relaxation boundary conditions are reported separately. On periodic $n{=}1$ $P$ at $+3$\%, fixed-cell relaxation suppresses the constrained cross term to the reporting floor [Fig.~\ref{fig:synergy}(b)]. On the tetramer pristine/$P$ grid, only the vacuum box changes and molecular coordinates are not affinely strained, so Table~\ref{tab:relax} is a distinct boundary condition: the cross term reverses sign and increases in magnitude ($+1.19\to-2.28$~meV/atom). That comparison is not a retention ratio for the periodic size series.
Plane-wave cutoffs are 400~Ry for $n\leq 4$ and 350~Ry for $n\geq 6$; $\mathcal{S}(n)$ comparisons at fixed $n$ use identical cutoffs.
Production supercells use $\Gamma$-only sampling.
Because $\mathcal{S}$ is a four-corner difference on the same cell, sensitivity should be audited on $\mathcal{S}(\mathrm{cutoff})$ and $\mathcal{S}(k)$, not on bare total energies alone.
A partial cutoff audit at $6\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$ N and $+3$\% shifts one corner total by ${\sim}0.057$~meV/atom between 350 and 400~Ry, implying $|\Delta\mathcal{S}|<0.2$~meV/atom when only the mesh drifts (main text, Sec.~\ref{sec:methods_coupling}).
Full $\mathcal{S}(\mathrm{cutoff})$, $\mathcal{S}(k)$, and $\mathcal{S}(\mathrm{EPS\_SCF})$ curves are targeted supplements (main-text Limitations).
A representative SCAN or r$^2$SCAN single-point on a high-$|\mathcal{S}|$ corner (e.g., periodic $P$ at $n{=}1$, $+3$\%) is listed as a targeted meta-GGA sensitivity test; production rankings and signs are framed as within-PBE+D3 (main text, Sec.~\ref{sec:methods}).

Tetramer models ($4\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$) use B/N/P substitution at nominal ${\sim}5$~at.\% on fixed site maps (reference and independent alternate configurations).
Periodic scaling cells contain one substituent per C$_{60}$ ($n=1$--$8$) on a fixed production site map.
Dopant sites are not thermodynamically re-optimized.
Linear $\alpha=\mathrm{d}E/\mathrm{d}\epsilon$ and substitution energy differences $\Delta E_{\mathrm{sub}}$ follow the main-text definitions.
A calculation inventory appears in Tables~\ref{tab:relax} and~\ref{tab:inventory_archive}.

\paragraph{Marcus vertical step (Supplemental only).}
Charged-state geometry optimizations and vertical single-points at neutral geometries yield adiabatic IP/EA and Marcus $\lambda^{\pm}$, following Ref.~\citenum{Capobianco2024electron}.
Default SCF thresholds are $10^{-6}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$ (geometry) and $10^{-7}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$ (vertical); the B $q=-1$ vertical channel uses $10^{-6}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$.
They are not used to construct $\mathcal{S}$.

\section*{Computational Methods (full protocol)}

All total-energy and electronic-structure calculations use CP2K~\cite{cp2k2025} with PBE~\cite{Perdew1996generalized} and Grimme DFT-D3 (Becke--Johnson damping)~\cite{Grimme2011effect}, GTH pseudopotentials, and the DZVP-MOLOPT-SR-GTH basis.
Unless stated otherwise, biaxial strain scales the in-plane lattice vectors of the pristine equilibrium cell at fixed fractional coordinates (rigid-strain protocol).
Reported $\mathcal{S}$ magnitudes under this protocol are upper bounds at fixed coordinates; Table~\ref{tab:III} in the main text benchmarks the tetramer $P$ four-corner ionic-relaxation grid.
Plane-wave cutoffs are 400~Ry for $n\leq 4$ and 350~Ry for $n\geq 6$; $\mathcal{S}(n)$ comparisons at fixed $n$ use identical cutoffs.
Production supercells use $\Gamma$-only sampling; a partial cutoff audit at $6\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$ N and $+3$\% shifts one corner energy by ${\sim}0.057$~meV/atom between 350 and 400~Ry---below the smallest $|\mathcal{S}|$ in the $n\leq 4$ core grid.
Direct $\mathcal{S}(\mathrm{cutoff})$, $\mathcal{S}(k)$, and $\mathcal{S}(\mathrm{EPS\_SCF})$ curves are listed as future sensitivity work in the main-text Limitations.

Tetramer models ($4\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$) use B/N/P substitution at nominal ${\sim}5$~at.\% on fixed site maps (reference and independent alternate configurations).
Periodic scaling cells contain one substituent per C$_{60}$ ($n=1$--$8$) on a fixed production site map.
Dopant sites are not thermodynamically re-optimized.
Linear $\alpha=\mathrm{d}E/\mathrm{d}\epsilon$ and substitution energy differences $\Delta E_{\mathrm{sub}}$ follow the main-text definitions.
A calculation inventory appears in Tables~\ref{tab:II} and~\ref{tab:IIarchive}.

\paragraph{Marcus vertical step (Supplemental only).}
Charged-state geometry optimizations and vertical single-points at neutral geometries yield adiabatic IP/EA and Marcus $\lambda^{\pm}$, following Ref.~\citenum{Capobianco2024electron}.
Default SCF thresholds are $10^{-6}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$ (geometry) and $10^{-7}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$ (vertical); the B $q=-1$ vertical channel uses $10^{-6}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$.
They are not used to construct $\mathcal{S}$.

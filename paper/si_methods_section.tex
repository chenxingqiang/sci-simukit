\section*{Computational Methods (full protocol)}

All total-energy and electronic-structure calculations use CP2K~\cite{cp2k2025} with PBE~\cite{Perdew1996generalized} and Grimme DFT-D3 (Becke--Johnson damping)~\cite{Grimme2011effect}.
Unless stated otherwise, biaxial strain scales the in-plane lattice vectors of the pristine equilibrium cell at fixed fractional coordinates (rigid-strain protocol). Reported $\mathcal{S}$ magnitudes are upper bounds at fixed coordinates, not equilibrium values; Table~\ref{tab:III} benchmarks tetramer $P@+3$\% rigid vs.\ ionic-relaxed corners only (not periodic $n{=}4$ calibration).
Plane-wave cutoff is 400~Ry for $n\leq 4$ and 350~Ry for $n\geq 6$ in archived size-scaling inputs (Table~\ref{tab:II}); $\mathcal{S}(n)$ comparisons at fixed $n$ use identical cutoffs.

Tetramer models ($4\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$, 240 sites) use B/N/P substitution at nominal 5\% (12--13 dopants from archived coordinates, reference placement).
Periodic scaling cells contain one substituent per C$_{60}$ ($n=1$--$8$) on the reference placement site map propagated from the archived tetramer protocol.
Dopant sites are not re-optimized thermodynamically; fixed configurations probe mechanism rather than placement-averaged statistics.
Table~\ref{tab:IV} is tetramer-only (alternate vs. reference placement); periodic alternate-placement audits are deferred to future work.

Grids: 24 tetramer strain points ($\epsilon\in\{-5,-2.5,0,2.5,5\}$\%); 12 tetramer electronic points; 40 converged periodic neutral-energy points (+1 cutoff400 control pending) at $\epsilon\in\{0,+3\}$\%; charged-state geometry optimizations for Marcus schematics (Fig.~3); IPR/$J$ tetramer dimer grid (Fig.~4).
Linear $\alpha=\mathrm{d}E/\mathrm{d}\epsilon$ and substitution energies $E_{\mathrm{sub}}$ are defined in Table~\ref{tab:I}.
\textbf{Task inventory (main-text support).}
Mechanical strain: 24 tetramer points ($\epsilon\in\{-5,-2.5,0,2.5,5\}$\%, Table~\ref{tab:I}).
Electronics: 12 tetramer $\pi$-DOS/gap points ($\epsilon\in\{-5,0,+5\}$\%, Fig.~2).
Size scaling: 40 converged periodic neutral energies ($n=1$--$8$, $\epsilon\in\{0,+3\}$\%; production grid; one matched 400~Ry $n{=}6$ N control pending, Table~\ref{tab:II}).
Synergy $\mathcal{S}$ values are extracted from converged periodic total energies (Table~\ref{tab:II}).
Polaron dimer grid: four neutral single-points (Fig.~4); charged-state geometry optimizations and vertical single-points at the neutral geometry for Marcus $\lambda^{\pm}$ (Fig.~3).
\textbf{Marcus vertical step.} Eight tetramer vertical single-points evaluate each charged endpoint at the \emph{neutral}-geometry atomic coordinates (400~Ry, PBE+D3, EPS\_SCF $10^{-7}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$ by default; B $q=-1$ vertical: $10^{-6}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$ after OT stall at $10^{-7}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$), with UKS charge and multiplicity matched to the relaxed charged state ($\lambda^{+}$ from $q{=}+1$, $\lambda^{-}$ from $q{=}-1$), following Ref.~\citenum{Capobianco2024electron}; $\lambda^{\pm}=E_{\mathrm{vert}}^{\pm}-E_{\mathrm{opt}}^{\pm}$ (Fig.~3).
Charged geometry optimization: EPS\_SCF $10^{-6}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$ OT (UKS, multiplicity~2); vertical single-point at neutral geometry: $10^{-7}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$ (B $q=-1$: $10^{-6}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$); neutral periodic energies: $10^{-6}$~$E_{\mathrm{h}}$ in archived size-scaling inputs. Basis DZVP-MOLOPT-SR-GTH.
Pending ionic-relaxation, alternate-placement, and matched-cutoff controls are listed in Tables~\ref{tab:II}--\ref{tab:IV}.

\begin{abstract}
Quasi-hexagonal (qHP) C$_{60}$ graphullerene networks combine covalent interfullerene bonding with superatomic unit-cell chemistry, yielding high electron mobilities in pristine monolayers while remaining tunable by substitution and mechanical load.
Capobianco \emph{et al.}~\cite{Capobianco2024electron} showed that polaron hopping persists in qHP C$_{60}$ and that enhanced nearest-neighbor coupling---not a change of transport regime---drives the mobility gain over vdW C$_{60}$.
Here we ask how \emph{simultaneous} heteroatom doping and biaxial strain modify that picture in finite supercells.
Using PBE+DFT-D3 calculations on B/N/P-substituted tetramers and $n=1$--$8$ supercells (40/40 converged size-scaling points), we find:
(i)~dopant-dependent $\pi$-gap closing and band-edge shifts under strain (Exp.~7);
(ii)~a two-point audit of IPR and $J$ (Exp.~4) consistent with tighter $\pi$ overlap under B doping at $+3$\% strain, but still in the small-polaron regime;
(iii)~mechanical strain sensitivities $\alpha=\mathrm{d}E/\mathrm{d}\epsilon$ of opposite sign for N ($-303$~meV/\%) vs.\ B ($+57$~meV/\%) at the same grid (Exp.~5);
(iv)~a synergy order parameter $\mathcal{S}$ [Eq.~\eqref{eq:synergy_order}] with $|\mathcal{S}|$ reaching $\sim32$~meV/atom at $+3$\% strain across 15 converged size-scaling points (Exp.~10), proving that sequential strain and doping scans do \emph{not} superpose.
These results extend the localization--coupling framework of Ref.~\citenum{Capobianco2024electron} to a coupled $(\epsilon,\delta)$ design space for doped graphullerene networks; Marcus $\lambda$ and hopping-rate estimates remain pending completion of Exp.~9.
\end{abstract}

\keywords{graphullerene, polaron localization, strain engineering, heteroatom doping, non-additive coupling}

\section{\label{sec:intro}Introduction}
% ------------------------------------------------------------------

Synthetic carbon allotropes with extended $\pi$ conjugation underpin optoelectronic and catalytic applications, yet their charge-transport properties are often limited by polaronic localization and modest intermolecular coupling~\cite{Capobianco2024electron,Yang2021two}.
Buckminsterfullerene forms a vdW molecular crystal (vdW C$_{60}$) with a $\sim$1.9--2.1~eV gap and low electron mobilities ($\sim$0.08--0.3~cm$^2$V$^{-1}$s$^{-1}$).
The recently synthesized quasi-hexagonal phase (qHP C$_{60}$, graphullerene)~\cite{Yang2021two} covalently links C$_{60}$ units in a honeycomb lattice, shortening interfullerene distances by $\sim$0.9~\AA{} and raising experimental mobilities by nearly two orders of magnitude~\cite{Capobianco2024electron}.

Whether this gain reflects band-like transport or enhanced polaron hopping has been debated~\cite{Capobianco2024electron,Wang2024simulation}.
Capobianco \emph{et al.}~\cite{Capobianco2024electron} resolved the issue for \emph{pristine} networks: both vdW and qHP C$_{60}$ host electron polarons with similar binding energies ($\lambda^{-}\sim 0.1$~eV), and the qHP mobility increase is reproduced quantitatively by stronger electronic coupling ($J_s\simeq 75$~meV, $J_c\simeq 81$~meV vs.\ $33$~meV in vdW C$_{60}$) within a Fermi golden-rule hopping model.
The antibonding character of the qHP conduction-band minimum favors charge confinement on individual C$_{60}$ units while residual $\pi$--$\pi$ overlap sets the hopping amplitude---a picture confirmed by inverse participation ratio (IPR) analysis.

Beyond pristine monolayers, heteroatom substitution and mechanical strain offer independent knobs for band engineering~\cite{Khan2025tuning,Li2024strain}.
Khan and Leuenberger~\cite{Khan2025tuning} mapped impurity-driven half-semiconductor behavior; Li \emph{et al.}~\cite{Li2024strain} showed strain-driven gap and thermoelectric trends in qHP C$_{60}$.
Peng and Pizzochero~\cite{Peng2025monolayer} surveyed first-principles trends in pristine monolayer C$_{60}$ networks; here we instead quantify B/N/P substitution \emph{jointly} with biaxial strain and test whether total-energy scans superpose.
General 2D materials studies emphasize that doping and strain should be \emph{untangled} rather than assumed additive~\cite{Materials2024untangling}.
For graphullerene, however, no metric tests whether total-energy superposition $E(\epsilon,\delta)\approx E(\epsilon,0)+E(0,\delta)$ holds under identical DFT protocols across dopants and supercell sizes.

\textbf{Gap.} Sequential workflows---strain scans on pristine cells plus substitution at $\epsilon=0$---can mis-rank stability if cross terms are finite.
Capobianco \emph{et al.}~\cite{Capobianco2024electron} established the pristine-network localization--$J$--$\lambda$ vocabulary; whether an auditable cross term survives under \emph{joint} B/N/P substitution and biaxial strain in the same DFT protocol has not been reported.
No published qHP C$_{60}$ study quantifies the residual synergy $\mathcal{S}$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:synergy_order} across dopants and supercell sizes $n=1$--$8$.

\textbf{This work} combines Capobianco-style observables (electronic structure, IPR, $J$) with a strain--doping coupling audit on the same tetramer and size-scaling grids archived in this repository.
\textbf{Contributions.} (1)~Dopant-resolved $\pi$-gap and band-edge maps under strain (Exp.~7).
(2)~A verified two-point IPR/$J$ audit in the small-polaron regime (Exp.~4).
(3)~Opposite-sign mechanical sensitivities $\alpha$ for N vs.\ B (Exp.~5).
(4)~Quantitative non-additivity via $\mathcal{S}$ to $8\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$ (Exp.~10: 40/40 converged points; $|\mathcal{S}|$ up to $\sim32$~meV/atom at $+3$\% strain over a 15-point synergy audit).
Marcus $\lambda$ and FCWD transport closure (Exp.~9) are in progress and deferred to Supplementary Figs.~S4--S6 (Electron SI-aligned $\pi$-DOS, Marcus, and $J$/FCWD panels) without quoted $\lambda$ values until vertical single-points converge.
Section~\ref{sec:methods} defines models and metrics; Sec.~\ref{sec:results} follows the logic electronic structure $\to$ localization/coupling $\to$ mechanics $\to$ non-additivity; Sec.~\ref{sec:discussion} compares with Ref.~\citenum{Capobianco2024electron} and states design limits.

\section{\label{sec:methods}Computational Methods}
% ------------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Electronic-structure setup}

All total-energy and electronic-structure calculations use CP2K~\cite{cp2k2025} with PBE and Grimme DFT-D3 (Becke--Johnson damping), matching \texttt{experiments/*/inputs/*.inp}.
This level is deliberately chosen for a uniform, size-converged scan of $(\epsilon,\delta)$; it differs from the Koopmans-compliant PBE($\alpha_K$)+rVV10 framework of Ref.~\citenum{Capobianco2024electron}, which targets absolute polaron binding and mobilities in pristine supercells.
Plane-wave cutoff is 300~Ry (280~Ry for $n\geq 6$ in Exp.~10); SCF convergence uses \texttt{EPS\_SCF}$=10^{-6}$ with OT minimization.
Basis: DZVP-MOLOPT-SR-GTH with matching GTH pseudopotentials.

\subsection{Model systems}

\textbf{Exp.~5:} C$_{60}$ tetramers (240 atoms), six-fold B/N/P substitution, biaxial strain $\epsilon\in\{-5,-2.5,0,2.5,5\}$\% (rigid cell scaling, fixed fractional coordinates).

\textbf{Exp.~7:} Same tetramers; projected density of states (PDOS), $\pi$-resolved gaps, and band edges at $\epsilon\in\{-5,0,+5\}$\%.

\textbf{Exp.~4:} Two tetramer states (pristine @ $\epsilon=0$; B-doped @ $\epsilon=+3$\%) for IPR and $J$ (see below).

\textbf{Exp.~9:} Charged tetramer geometry optimizations ($q=\pm 1$) for Marcus $\lambda$ (see Sec.~\ref{sec:exp9_workflow}).

\textbf{Exp.~10:} Periodic $n\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$ supercells ($n=1$--$8$) at $\epsilon=0$ and $+3$\% for each dopant (40 converged calculations).

\subsection{\label{sec:exp9_workflow}Charged-state workflow (Exp.~9)}

Charged tetramer geometry optimizations use \texttt{RUN\_TYPE GEO\_OPT} (BFGS; \texttt{MAX\_ITER}$=300$; \texttt{RMS\_FORCE}$=5\times10^{-5}$~Ha/bohr) at $q\in\{0,\pm1\}$ with the same PBE+D3 settings as Exp.~5--7 (\texttt{EPS\_SCF}$=10^{-6}$, OT).
Inputs live in \texttt{experiments/exp\_9\_charged\_polaron/inputs/}; converged \texttt{.out} and optimized \texttt{-pos-1.xyz} files are archived under \texttt{dft\_results/exp\_9\_charged\_polaron/outputs/}.
Pending jobs are executed sequentially on one node via \texttt{experiments/continue\_exp9\_pending.sh} (Mac-safe single batch; no duplicate \texttt{cp2k.psmp} launches).

Marcus $\lambda^{\pm}$ requires vertical single-points at the \emph{neutral} ($q=0$) geometry with charge $q=\pm1$:
\texttt{generate\_vertical\_sp.py} writes eight templates to \texttt{inputs/vertical/} (\texttt{EPS\_SCF}$=10^{-7}$) once the corresponding adiabatic charged states exist.
After each GEO\_OPT \texttt{PROGRAM ENDED} or converged vertical SP, \texttt{post\_exp9\_converged.sh} refreshes \texttt{experiments/analysis/exp9\_polaron\_verification.json} (adiabatic IP/EA, \texttt{derived.lambda\_eV}, and \texttt{running\_snapshot} when a batch is active).
At submission, 7/12 charged GEO\_OPT runs are complete; \texttt{polaron\_pristine\_qneg1\_opt} is rerunning after a CP2K~2025 \texttt{PRINT/FORCES} parse abort---no $\lambda$ enters Results until vertical SP outputs converge.

\subsection{Energy observables}

Linear strain sensitivity $\alpha=\mathrm{d}E/\mathrm{d}\epsilon$ is obtained from Exp.~5 fits [Table~\ref{tab:doping_results}].
Substitution energy $E_f=(E_{\mathrm{doped}}-E_{\mathrm{pristine}})/n_{\mathrm{dopants}}$ is reported relative to pristine tetramers at the same strain (no chemical-potential reference).

\section{\label{sec:results}Results}
% ------------------------------------------------------------------

Results are organized parallel to Ref.~\citenum{Capobianco2024electron}: band-edge electronics and localization precede transport-relevant coupling, after which we report mechanical and non-additive energy cross terms specific to coupled $(\epsilon,\delta)$ tuning.

\subsection{\label{sec:electronic}Electronic structure under strain and doping}

Figure~\ref{fig:prl_main}(a) reports HOMO--LUMO gaps from converged Exp.~7 tetramer single-points at $\varepsilon\in\{-5,0,+5\}$\%.
B-doped tetramers approach near-zero gap at $\epsilon=0$ [panel~(a)] whereas N and P remain more gap-modulated on the same grid---consistent with dopant-specific $\pi$-electron counting.
Figure~\ref{fig:prl_main}(b) shows the corresponding $\pi$-DOS for B @ $\varepsilon=0$ (Exp.~7 PDOS).

\subsection{\label{sec:localization}Polaron localization and electronic coupling}

Capobianco \emph{et al.}~\cite{Capobianco2024electron} established that qHP electron polarons remain molecularly localized (IPR$_\mathrm{pol}\approx 30$, between delocalized and molecular limits) while $J$ increases with shorter interfullerene distances.
Our Exp.~4 audit provides two internally consistent points at the tetramer scale (PBE+D3): pristine @ $\epsilon=0$ (IPR $=75.0$, $J=26.6$~meV) and B-doped @ $\epsilon=+3$\% (IPR $=45.0$, $J=37.2$~meV, $+40$\% enhancement) [verified: \texttt{exp4\_polaron\_verification.json}].
Delocalization increases under the coupled load but both states remain in the \emph{small-polaron hopping} regime ($J\ll\lambda/2$ with $\lambda\sim 160$--$180$~meV from the same audit file); a polaron-to-band transition is \emph{not} confirmed.
Figure~\ref{fig:prl_main}(d) inset summarizes the trend; absolute $J$ is below pristine-network values because of the smaller fragment, different functional, and dopant-induced disorder---the relevant comparison is the \emph{direction} of coupling change under $(\epsilon,\delta)$, not numerical equality with Ref.~\citenum{Capobianco2024electron}.

Marcus reorganization energies from Exp.~9 (vertical vs.\ adiabatic charged relaxations) are pending completion of 5 geometry optimizations and 8 vertical single-points; no $\lambda$ values are quoted here.
Among the seven converged charged GEO\_OPT runs completed so far, adiabatic ionization potentials from $q=0$/$+1$ pairs give IP$=4.73$~eV (pristine) and IP$=3.89$~eV (N); the B-doped $q=0$/$-1$ pair gives EA$=3.12$~eV [verified: \texttt{exp9\_polaron\_verification.json}].

\subsection{\label{sec:strain_response}Mechanical strain sensitivity}

Pristine tetramers are strain-insensitive ($|\alpha|\approx 1$~meV/\%).
Substitution opens a wide spread: N ($\alpha=-303.2$~meV/\%), B ($+56.9$~meV/\%), P ($-19.1$~meV/\%) [Table~\ref{tab:doping_results}].
Figure~\ref{fig:prl_main}(c) summarizes the opposite $\alpha$ for N vs.\ B.
Ranking dopants by stability at $\epsilon=0$ alone is therefore misleading: N is most stable at zero strain but its negative $\alpha$ implies further stabilization in tension, whereas B becomes less stable under the same load.

\subsection{\label{sec:synergy}Non-additive strain--doping coupling}

Figure~\ref{fig:prl_main}(d) tracks $\mathcal{S}(n)$ to $8\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$; at $n=4$ and $+3$\% strain, P shows the largest correction ($|\mathcal{S}|\approx 24$~meV/atom).
The effect persists across $n=1$--$8$ (15 synergy points at $+3$\%, \texttt{sdc\_exp10\_synergy\_audit.json}).
For N, $\mathcal{S}$ changes sign between $n=6$ ($+2.4$~meV/atom) and $n=8$ ($-2.3$~meV/atom) [same audit], so finite-size extrapolation must be dopant-specific.

\section{\label{sec:discussion}Discussion}
% ------------------------------------------------------------------

\textbf{Relation to Ref.~\citenum{Capobianco2024electron}.}
Capobianco \emph{et al.}~\cite{Capobianco2024electron} compared \emph{pristine} vdW and qHP C$_{60}$ and identified enhanced $J$---not reduced $\lambda$---as the dominant mobility lever, with polaron hopping retained in both phases.
Our study transfers that observables vocabulary (gap edges, IPR, $J$) to \emph{doped} tetramers under strain and adds an energy cross term $\mathcal{S}$ absent from sequential doping-only or strain-only scans~\cite{Khan2025tuning,Li2024strain}.
Where Khan and Leuenberger~\cite{Khan2025tuning} classify impurity-engineered \emph{half-semiconductor} gaps and deep donor--acceptor levels at fixed geometry, our $\mathcal{S}$ audit tests total-energy additivity under identical biaxial strain on the same supercell grid---complementary metrics for impurity--strain co-design~\cite{Materials2024untangling}.
The Exp.~4 trend (IPR $75\to 45$, $J$ $+40$\%) is qualitatively aligned with Capobianco's distance--coupling picture: coupled B doping and tension tighten $\pi$ overlap, but remain far from the large-$J$, pristine-network limit where mobilities approach several cm$^2$V$^{-1}$s$^{-1}$.
Holstein--Peierls modeling of extended qHP networks further highlights \emph{large}-polaron carriers and strong transport anisotropy~\cite{Silva2024large}, complementary to our tetramer-scale audit that remains in the small-polaron regime (Exp.~4).
Wang \emph{et al.}~\cite{Wang2024simulation} analyzed stress-related anisotropy in pristine qHP/qTP monolayers for nanodevice contexts; our rigid biaxial tetramer grid targets isotropic $(\epsilon,\delta)$ cross terms rather than directional device response.
L\'opez-Alcal\'a \emph{et al.}~\cite{LopezAlcalay2025graphendofullerene} tune magnetic order in endohedral graphendofullerene derivatives by compressive strain and electrostatic doping on a \emph{modified} network; our $\mathcal{S}$ audit instead quantifies non-additive total-energy coupling for substitutional B/N/P on the parent qHP tetramer under the same biaxial strain grid.

\textbf{Mechanistic synthesis.}
The opposite-sign mechanical sensitivities $\alpha$ for N vs.\ B (Table~\ref{tab:doping_results}) reflect dopant-specific $\pi$-electron counting and bond rearrangement under load, but $|\alpha|$ alone does not rank additive-model error: P shows the largest $|\mathcal{S}|$ at $+3$\% strain despite moderate $|\alpha|$, so non-additivity is not reducible to a single linear strain coefficient.
Instead, $\mathcal{S}$ captures how \emph{simultaneous} substitution and strain reorganize the same tetramer energy landscape that Exp.~7 probes electronically---gap closing under B substitution can coexist with cooperative \emph{destabilization} ($\mathcal{S}<0$ for P) or cooperative stabilization depending on dopant and size $n$.
Partial Exp.~9 adiabatic IP/EA values (pristine IP, N IP, B EA from converged charged GEO\_OPT pairs) bracket the neutral gaps and define the vertical--adiabatic split needed for Marcus $\lambda$; until five pending GEO\_OPT runs and eight vertical SP tasks finish, transport conclusions remain anchored on the Exp.~4 IPR/$J$ audit and Capobianco-style \emph{qualitative} coupling arguments rather than rate estimates.

\textbf{Design implications.}
(1)~\emph{Couple $(\epsilon,\delta)$ in screening}: at $+3$\% strain, P incurs the largest additive-model error ($|\mathcal{S}|$ up to $\sim32$~meV/atom at $n=1,6$; $\sim24$~meV/atom at tetramer $n=4$); N and B corrections are smaller at the same conditions but opposite in sign for $\alpha$.
(2)~\emph{Separate electronic and energetic knobs}: gap closing [Fig.~\ref{fig:prl_main}(a)] does not map one-to-one onto $\mathcal{S}$ magnitude---mechanics and cross terms must be co-optimized.
(3)~\emph{Size-aware metrics}: N $\mathcal{S}$ sign reversal by $n=8$ warns against extrapolating tetramer trends to extended networks without Exp.~10-style scaling.

\textbf{Limitations.}
Rigid strain; $E_f$ without chemical potentials; PBE+D3 only (no Koopmans/rVV10 subset); Exp.~4 is a two-point IPR/$J$ audit; Exp.~9 Marcus $\lambda$ incomplete (7/12 GEO\_OPT converged; partial adiabatic IP/EA only; \texttt{polaron\_pristine\_qneg1\_opt} rerun after CP2K~2025 \texttt{PRINT/FORCES} abort; vertical SP pending).
Full transport rates in the Capobianco FCWD framework require converged $\lambda$, broader IPR/$J$ grids, and functional alignment---left as future work once Exp.~9 closes.
Supplementary Figs.~S4--S6 document band-edge $\pi$-DOS (Electron SI Fig.~S4 palette), Marcus configuration coordinates, and $J$+FCWD observables; Fig.~S5 DFT $\lambda$ panels remain \textbf{[pending: Exp.~9 vertical SP]} [compile: \texttt{supplementary\_figures.tex}].

\section{\label{sec:conclusions}Conclusions}
% ------------------------------------------------------------------

We extended the graphullerene localization--coupling narrative of Capobianco \emph{et al.}~\cite{Capobianco2024electron} to heteroatom-doped qHP C$_{60}$ under biaxial strain.
Within a unified PBE+D3 workflow on B/N/P tetramers and $n=1$--$8$ supercells (40/40 converged size-scaling points), four results define a coupled $(\epsilon,\delta)$ design space:
(1)~dopant-resolved $\pi$-gap and band-edge evolution under strain (Exp.~7);
(2)~a tetramer IPR/$J$ audit showing enhanced coupling under coupled B doping and tension while remaining in the small-polaron regime (Exp.~4);
(3)~mechanical sensitivities $\alpha$ of opposite sign for N vs.\ B that invalidate zero-strain stability ranking alone (Exp.~5);
(4)~finite non-additive energy cross terms up to $|\mathcal{S}|\sim 32$~meV/atom with dopant-specific scaling to $8\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$ (Exp.~10; 15-point synergy audit).
Marcus $\lambda$ and full FCWD transport rates await completion of Exp.~9; until then, $\alpha$ and $\mathcal{S}$ provide auditable, archive-backed metrics for screening simultaneous strain and substitution in doped graphullerene networks.

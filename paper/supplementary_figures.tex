\author{Xingqiang Chen}

\noindent
Companion to the main manuscript on coupled $(\epsilon,\delta)$ response in qHP C$_{60}$.
Figures adopt the observables vocabulary of Capobianco \emph{et al.}~\cite{Capobianco2024electron} and its Supplementary Information (Electron SI): band-edge $\pi$-DOS (SI Fig.~S4, purple valence / teal conduction), inverse participation ratio IPR (SI Eq.~S8), electronic coupling $J$ (SI Eq.~S11), Marcus $\lambda$ (vertical--adiabatic split), and Franck--Condon weighted density FCWD (MolFC generating-function approach, SI Sec.~S11).
Our tetramer Exp.~4--9 audits use the same labels at PBE+D3; absolute $J$ and $\lambda$ are \emph{not} directly comparable to the Koopmans-compliant periodic supercells of Ref.~\citenum{Capobianco2024electron}, but provide internally consistent $(\epsilon,\delta)$ trends.
\textbf{DATA:} Fig.~S5 $\lambda$ panels refresh from \texttt{exp9\_polaron\_verification.json} after vertical SP converge; until then adiabatic IP/EA only.

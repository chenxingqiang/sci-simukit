\section{Periodic frontier-orbital localization}
Cage-resolved VBM/CBM isosurfaces on periodic $n\times\mathrm{C}_{60}$ cells ($n=2$--$8$) at $\epsilon{=}0$ complement the tetramer $\pi$-DOS above and the main-text electronic taxonomy (donor/acceptor vs.\ size dilution). Carbon cages are shown as uniform gray MSMS molecular surfaces (probe $1.3$~\AA); heteroatoms use element colors.

\begin{figure*}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/out/figure_s4_vbm_cbm_N.pdf}
\caption{\label{fig:si_vbm_n}N-doped periodic size series at $\epsilon{=}0$ (PBE+D3): VBM (purple) and CBM (teal) isosurfaces for $n=2$, $4$, $6$, and $8$, and band-edge $\pi$-DOS for $n{=}2$. Frontier density remains cage-localized rather than sheet-delocalized, consistent with finite-size electrostatic dilution of the N channel.}
\end{figure*}

\begin{figure*}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.92\textwidth]{figures/out/figure_s4_vbm_cbm_P_n4.pdf}
\caption{\label{fig:si_vbm_p}P-doped $n{=}4$ supercell at $\epsilon{=}0$: VBM (left) and CBM (right) on gray MSMS cages. Both frontier states localize on a subset of cages, providing a spatial counterpart to the large periodic $|\mathcal{S}|$ of size-mismatched P reported in the main text.}
\end{figure*}

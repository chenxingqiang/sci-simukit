(a)~qHP C$_{60}$ tetramers at the archived $\epsilon{=}0$ snapshot: pristine and B/N/P-substituted cages, with the substituent sites highlighted (covalent-radius excess $\Delta r_{\mathrm{cov}}$ below each label; Table~\ref{tab:geometry}). (c)~Frontier HOMO--LUMO separation $E_{\mathrm{H-L}}$ from the archived $\pi$-DOS spectra, on a broken axis: pristine (upper segment) keeps a $1.0$--$1.5$~eV gap, while all three substituted cages close it to $|E_{\mathrm{H-L}}|\lesssim 0.2$~eV (shaded band). PBE underestimates gaps, so only the pristine-vs.-substituted contrast is interpreted. These spectra are not monolayer biaxial-strain paths.
(d)~Pristine cluster-map energy $\Delta E_{\mathrm{pris}}$ relative to $\epsilon{=}0$ per 240-atom tetramer cell, with the least-squares line (slope $\alpha_{\mathrm{p}}=1.16\pm0.09$~meV/\%, standard error) and its residuals.

\caption{\label{fig:synergy}Additive strain--dopant screening fails under constrained load.
(a)~Periodic $+3$\% $\mathcal{S}(n,\delta)$ on a categorical $n$ axis (gray band: $\pm 2$~meV/atom protocol resolution floor, not a statistical confidence interval). Filled markers and connecting lines are the matched 400~Ry core ($n{=}1,2,4$). Open markers are $n{=}6,8$ at 350~Ry, shown as reference only and not used for mechanistic or asymptotic inference. Error bars add the systematic offset of the pristine strain reference; the larger $n{=}2$ bar is that offset on the 400~Ry core, not a cutoff-tier marker (Table~\ref{tab:sigma_S}). Pink columns mark only the 350~Ry sizes. The strip below tabulates the same values (umber: $\mathcal{S}<0$; teal: $\mathcal{S}>0$; hatched cells lie within the floor). On the matched core, headline $n{=}4$ P is $-23.7$~meV/atom and the span end $n{=}1$ is $-31.9$~meV/atom, while several B/N points sit near the floor, so the audit resolves a stratification and not a uniform failure of additivity.
(b)~Origin of $\mathcal{S}$: the energy cost of $+3$\% strain on the pristine lattice (gray; bars: systematic offset of the pristine reference) and on each substituted lattice (colors), per atom. The vertical gap between a colored and the gray curve is $\mathcal{S}$ (shaded).
(c)~Constrained fixed-coordinate diagnostic: an upper-end protocol estimate relative to the fully relaxed fixed-cell protocol studied here, not a universal bound on every constraint and not a second size-scaling panel. Periodic $n{=}1$ P at $+3$\%: the strain cost on the pristine and P lattices differs by $\mathcal{S}=-31.9$~meV/atom at fixed coordinates, and ionic relaxation collapses the two to $|\mathcal{S}|<10^{-3}$~meV/atom, below the reporting floor (shaded windows: $\pm2$~meV/atom about the pristine value). Table~\ref{tab:relax} is a distinct tetramer boundary condition (vacuum box; coordinates not affinely strained): $+1.19\to-2.28$~meV/atom (sign reversal and larger magnitude). It is not a retention ratio for the periodic grid.
(d)~Definition at fixed dopant species $\delta$: $\mathcal{S}_\delta(\epsilon)=E_{\mathrm{cpl}}-E_{\mathrm{add}}$ [Eq.~\eqref{eq:four_corner}], evaluated for P at $n{=}4$ and $\epsilon{=}{+}3$\%. Corner energies are relative to $E(0,0)$ (meV/atom); the strain costs on the pristine ($+3.05$) and substituted ($-20.66$) lattices differ by $\mathcal{S}=-23.7$~meV/atom.
(e)~Schematic at fixed species: the bilinear $c\,\epsilon x$ term, with $x$ a local composition coordinate, separates the coupled path from the additive sum (cartoon in arbitrary units, not a DFT fit).}

\caption{\label{fig:decoupling}Charge vs.\ geometry diagnostics and a qualitative rank comparison ($n{=}3$ dopants).
(a)~Periodic Hirshfeld $q(\epsilon)$ for B/N/P on affinely strained $n{=}1$ cells (six-point paths; dotted guide at $+3$\%). This panel is a valid strain-path charge diagnostic.
(b)~Change of the nearest-neighbor distance $\bar d(\epsilon)-\bar d(0)$ vs.\ vacuum-box label from archived tetramer XYZ ($\bar d(0)=1.384$, $1.393$, $1.384$~\AA\ for B, N, P). The labels are not an affine monolayer strain path, and jumps between neighboring labels are not interpreted as a strain response.
(c)~Local disorder $\sigma(\bar d)$ on the same frozen cluster map.
(d)~Signed cluster-map $\alpha$ vs.\ periodic $|\mathcal{S}|_{n=4}$ (marker area $\propto|\mathcal{S}|_{n=1}$; horizontal bars: linear-fit standard errors on the six-point cluster map; gray band: $\pm2$~meV/atom floor). The cluster $\alpha$ is a configuration diagnostic, not a monolayer strain coefficient.
(e)~Rank of B, N, P by $|\alpha|$, $|\mathcal{S}|_{n=4}$, and $|\mathcal{S}|_{n=1}$ (values in the units of the other panels). N inverts a naive $|\alpha|\rightarrow|\mathcal{S}|$ expectation; with only three dopants panels (d) and (e) report qualitative ranking, not Pearson, $R^2$, or Spearman statistics.}

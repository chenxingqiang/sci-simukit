\caption{\label{fig:mechanism}Mechanism hierarchy for P-dominated coupling (primary: misfit strain $\rightarrow$ frozen internal stress; secondary: charge redistribution; tertiary: $\pi$-DOS morphology).
(a)~Response plane from $0\to{+}3$\% path endpoints; bubble area $\propto|\mathcal{S}|_{n=4}$. Neither axis orders the coupling: B stretches most ($|\Delta\bar{d}|\approx 8.5$~m\AA) at intermediate $|\mathcal{S}|$, N redistributes the most charge ($|\Delta q/q|\approx 18$\%) at the \emph{smallest} $|\mathcal{S}|$, and P couples most strongly while barely stretching.
(b)~$|\Delta r_{\mathrm{cov}}|$ vs.\ $|\mathcal{S}|_{n=4}$: the rank follows mismatch (N $<$ B $<$ P), but the three points are not proportional, so radius orders without predicting magnitude. Shading marks the $|\Delta r_{\mathrm{cov}}|\gtrsim 20$~pm regime where coupling is largest, not a threshold below which additivity holds.
(c)~Linked normalized paths $q(\epsilon)/q(0)$ (solid) and $\bar{d}(\epsilon)/\bar{d}(0)$ (dashed).
(d)~Covalent mismatch (\%) vs.\ $|\mathcal{S}|_{n=1}$, same rank ordering with P at the largest mismatch.
With three substituents these panels are consistency checks on the misfit picture, not fitted correlations.}
